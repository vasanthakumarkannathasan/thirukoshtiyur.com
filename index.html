About Thirukoshtiyur Temple

Sacred Abode of Lord Sowmiya Narayana Perumal

108 Divya Desam

One of the 108 sacred Vishnu temples praised in Nalayira Divya Prabandham

Unique Architecture

96 feet high Ashtanga Vimanam with 8 multi-tiered towers

Ramanuja's Legacy

Sacred place where Ramanuja learned the divine Ashtachara Mantram

The Arulmigu Sowmiya Narayana Perumal Temple at Thirukoshtiyur is one of the most revered shrines among the 108 Divya Desams. Known as the "Southern Badrinath," this ancient temple is famous for its unique architectural marvel where the deity appears in all three postures - standing, sitting, and sleeping - a rare phenomenon found in only a few temples worldwide.

The temple holds immense significance in Sri Vaishnava tradition as the sacred place where Sri Ramanuja, after visiting 18 times, received the divine Ashtachara Mantram from Thirukoshtiyur Nambi and subsequently preached it to humanity from the temple tower.

Temple Specialities

Unique Features of the Divine Abode

Main Deity

Lord Sowmiya Narayana Perumal

The presiding deity with divine consort Thirumagal Nachiyar blesses devotees in three divine postures across different levels of the temple - a unique architectural and spiritual marvel.

  • Ground Level: Krishna in dancing posture (Boologam - Earth)
  • Middle Level: Sayana Thirukkolam - Narayana resting on Adi Sesha
  • Top Level: Sowmya Narayana Perumal in standing posture (Vaikundham)
Spiritual Significance

Divine Benefits

Devotees who worship at this sacred temple are blessed with:

  • Resolution of marriage hindrances
  • Blessings for progeny (children)
  • Success in business and career
  • Relief from health issues
  • Spiritual enlightenment and peace

Women worshipping with lamp (villaku) are blessed with good husband and children. The temple is also known as "Aayarpaadi" in Divya Prabandham.

Temple History

Ancient Legacy and Sacred Stories

Origin of Thirukoshtiyur

In ancient times, a powerful demon named Hiranyan terrorized sages, devas, and people across the realms. Kadambha Maharishi's Ashram became a safe refuge where rishis, yogis, and devas gathered seeking divine protection.

Lord Brahma and all devas assembled and discussed with Sriman Narayana at this sacred place. The Lord decided to incarnate as Narasimha (half-lion, half-human) to destroy the demon and restore dharma.

Since multiple deities gathered and discussed ("Goshti" = assembly) here, the place came to be known as "Goshtiyur" or "Thirukoshtiyur".

Ramanuja's Divine Connection

The temple gained immense significance when Sri Ramanuja, the great Vaishnavite Acharya, visited this temple 18 times from Srirangam to learn the sacred Ashtachara Mantram ("Om Namo Narayanaya") from Thirukoshtiyur Nambi.

Initially rejected 17 times, Ramanuja was finally accepted on his 18th visit when he showed true humility. After receiving the divine mantra, Nambi warned him not to preach it to others, lest he go to hell.

Choosing to sacrifice his own salvation for humanity's benefit, Ramanuja climbed the temple tower and preached the sacred mantra to all. Moved by this selfless act, Nambi blessed him, calling him "Emberumanar" (The Great Master).

How to Reach

By Bus

Regular bus services available from Sivagangai, Thiruppathur, Karaikudi, Madurai, and Trichy. Buses run every 10-15 minutes from nearby towns.

By Train

Nearest railway stations: Karaikudi (closest), Sivagangai, and Madurai. From stations, take connecting buses to Thirukoshtiyur.

By Air

Nearest airports: Trichy Airport (closest, ~80 km) and Madurai Airport (~100 km). Hire taxis or take buses from airports.
